Pointee directions · round seven

Ten ways Pointee could look

The rebrand away from Claude Code Probe. Each direction below is a complete visual identity for the extension: its accent, its surfaces, its type, and every piece of chrome — the hover outline, the info label, the toolbar, the edit panel with its tether, the colour picker, the toast, the settings page, the icon — rendered at real scale in both its dark and its light theme, over a light page and over a dark one.

Fixed in every direction

  • Hairline borders define surfaces; shadows stay soft and short.
  • Translucent panels let the page ghost through a blur.
  • Near-neutral surfaces. Colour appears only where it means something.
  • Monoline icons at one weight, and keyboard hints you can see.
  • Geist Sans + Geist Mono at 11 / 12 / 13px, 10px for tracked micro-labels.

Varied across the ten

  • Accent — and the icon that carries it.
  • Dark surface tone — pure black, cool near-black, warm graphite.
  • Radius — tight 4 to 6, or soft 10 to 16.
  • Selection — ring, brackets, glow, slow ants, halo, tint.
  • Toolbar — icon pill, labelled segments, command bar.
  • Panel — floating, docked, attached to the element.
  • Label — stacked rows, chips, two columns.
  • Settings — spec sheet, sidebar, one column.
  • Motion — restrained ease, or a spring. Press replay on a stage.

How to answer

  • Name a number. 06 is an answer.
  • Or a hybrid: 03’s material with 06’s toolbar is an answer too.
  • Contrast is printed under every text token. Anything under 4.5 : 1 is marked, and there should be none.
  • Nothing here is a picture. The chrome is real CSS driven by a token block per direction; the winner’s block becomes tokens.css.

Round seven of this repo’s design rounds, and the first that starts from the brand rather than one surface. The six before it live beside this file in test/. When one of these wins — or a hybrid does — its handle is retired, its values move into tokens.css, and this page stays as the record of what was on the table.
