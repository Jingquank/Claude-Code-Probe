Direction 07, refined

Light-first, soft corners, an attached panel, a pill toolbar, slow ants, a spring under every entrance — kept. Changed: the accent is now a red-orange, shown in three shades so the exact hue is yours to pick; the neutrals warmed to follow it; each theme sits on its own kind of page; the mark is a blank until you draw one. Added: the motion, playable.

Kept from 07

  • Light designed first, dark derived second.
  • Radius 10 / 14, blur 16, hairline borders.
  • Pill toolbar, attached panel, stacked-row label, sidebar settings.
  • Slow ants for the selection.

Changed

  • Accent — red-orange. Vermilion, Tomato or Persimmon: pick one below.
  • Neutrals — warm-tinted instead of green-tinted, so they lean toward the accent.
  • Danger — moved to a crimson so it cannot be mistaken for the accent.
  • Pairing — light chrome over light pages, dark over dark. System follows the OS.
  • Icon — yours, later. The slot is empty on purpose.

How to answer

  • A shade: A, B or C.
  • A motion preset, after playing the stage: soft, snappy, bouncy, or restrained.
  • A handle for the direction, since Mint no longer fits.

The motion, played

One flow on the real chrome: the hover outline glides from card to card, the selection settles in with the label and toolbar a beat apart, Edit swaps them for the attached panel whose ticks grow out one by one, Copy presses and the toast arrives, then everything leaves faster than it came. Entering takes the preset's curve; leaving is always a short ease, because a departure should never bounce. Four presets, each with the spring it maps to in motion. Slow it three times to see the overshoot.
