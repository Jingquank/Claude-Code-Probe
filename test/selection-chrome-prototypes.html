Pointee selection chrome · round one

Fourteen shapes for the selection chrome

The info label and the toolbar, redesigned. Today they are two boxes of unrelated widths bracketing the element, and the toolbar mixes four registers in one strip. Each direction below is a structure — how the readout and the actions relate to each other and to the element — drawn three ways at real scale in the shipped Vermilion tokens: hover beside selected, the hard case from the screenshot, and an element with no room above it.

Fixed in every direction

  • The four actions, with their names: Copy Code, Screenshot, Edit, Select Parent.
  • Keyboard hints visible somewhere: Option to measure, Escape to step back.
  • All five readout lines at rest: identity, text, layout, paint, breadcrumb.
  • The shipped tokens and type: Vermilion, Geist, 13 / 12 / 11 / 10.

Varied across the fourteen

  • Structure — one card, two pieces, beside, corner, tab, rail, command line, dock, overlay, ribbon.
  • Actions — icon + label, icons with tooltips, text with dividers, one primary, a vertical list.
  • Hints — in the action row, a caption, the identity row, the breadcrumb row, under a rail.
  • Breadcrumb — marquee, clamped, wrapped, trimmed.
  • Outline and material — 2px ants on glass, 1.5px ants, a 1px ring, opaque, flat.

How to answer

  • Name a number. 08 is an answer.
  • Or a hybrid: 01's card with 08's action bar is an answer too.
  • The winner's structure decides how much of the placement solver is revised; its action register decides the toolbar markup.

Round one for the selection chrome. The brief is docs/SELECTION-CHROME-ROUND.md; when a shape wins, its handle is retired and the label and toolbar are rebuilt to it, with test/placement.mjs re-swept for whatever piece count it has.
